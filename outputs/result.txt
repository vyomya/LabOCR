Project: Li electrodeposition - glyme electrolytes

June 4
Goal: Screen electrolyte 240604-B for stable Li plating at 30°C
Electrolyte: 1M LiTFSI in diglyme: EtOH (4:1 v/v)
20 mL tot

Add 8% 5 mol% 12-crown-4 as additive. Stir 20 min
→ T @ 22.4C, glovebox H₂O < 1 ppm
Working elec.: glassy C RDE (0.3 cm²); CE: Li foil;
ref: Ag/AgCl

Deposition run 240604-B1
Apply -0.45V vs Ag/AgCl, 90 min, ω = 1600 rpm
J = 0.50 mA/cm² and 0.3 cm²

$$\frac{0.5 \text{ mA}}{\text{cm}^2} \cdot 0.3 \text{ cm}^2 = 1.5 \text{ E-4 A}$$

t = 90 min = 5400 s

Q = 1.5 E-4 A · 5400 s = 0.81 A·s = 0.81 C

n = $\frac{Q}{zF}$ = $\frac{0.81 \text{ C}}{96485 \text{ C/mol}}$ = $8.4 \text{ E-6 mol}$ $\frac{6.94 \text{ g Li}}{\text{mol Li}}$ = $5.8 \text{ E-5}$

$$1 e^- = 1 Li^+$$
