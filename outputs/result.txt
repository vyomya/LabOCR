1. Dissolve Al₂O₃ in 10 mL ethanol
2. Sonicate Al₂O₃ sol'n until dissolved
3. Dissolve Mn in 8 mL ethanol
4. Stir Mn sol'n until dissolved
5. Check stability of sol'n
6. Stir Al₂O₃ for 30 min
7. 1 wt%: 100 mg Mn → add 8 mL of Mn sol'n to Al₂O₃ sol'n
8. Stir 30 min more
9. Remove stir bar
10. Rotovap at 60°C, 100 rpm, 160 mbar for 20 min
11. Oven at 60°C - 80°C overnight (~24 hrs in oven)
12. Actual Mn sol'n to Al₂O₃ sol'n

10 wt% = 91.3 mg
5 wt% = 228.6 mg
